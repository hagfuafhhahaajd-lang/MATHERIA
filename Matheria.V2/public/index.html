RESULTS

The game is playable end to end: a player registers, picks a grade, answers randomly generated math questions, fights monsters and bosses, earns gold, upgrades their hero, and can compare gold on the online leaderboard or fight a boss together with a friend. The grade sets which operations a player sees, so a younger child gets simple addition and subtraction while an older student gets multiplication, division, roots and equations. The next step is testing with real students to measure whether it helps them practise.
